## 16. 007 Nightfire / Agent Under Fire

When you are a young boy, James Bond is just about the coolest dude on the planet. He drives fast cars, saves the world, gets the hottest chicks & always has a sharp line to hit you with before he ices you. I haven't played First Light yet, but the PS2 era James Bond games here did a great job of helping you feel like Bond, rewarding you with 'Bond Moments' that make you feel like the super spy himself. The levels usually have multiple routes you could take, the car missions felt fun back then (20 years ago, so this could just be rose-colored glasses) and the local multiplayer was awesome. Agent Under Fire's multiplayer let you change a bunch of the settings, such as gravity and movement speed, so you could turn the game into a ridiculous deathmatch between gods & superheroes who could jump 50 feet in the air and move with super-speed. Nightfire, while lacking those settings, allowed you to play the multiplayer against bots, so you could practice your skills while waiting for your buddies to come over on the weekends.
